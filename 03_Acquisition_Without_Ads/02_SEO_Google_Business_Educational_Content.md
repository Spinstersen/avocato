# Acquisition #2: SEO & Google Business Profile (Be Found When They Search)

> **Best for introverts who hate daily social. Slow start (30-60 days) but then passive leads forever. 100% compliant.**

## Why SEO is Legal Gold
Client searches: "modèle contrat prestation freelance Maroc" or "CGV e-commerce Maroc obligatoire" or "avocat loi 09-08 [ville]".
If you wrote the article, Google shows YOU. They contact YOU. That's not démarchage, that's being helpful. No ad needed.

## 1. Google Business Profile (Fiche d'établissement) — Do TODAY (30 min)

Even with small office, create/optimize:
- Name: "Me [Nom] — Avocat Droit de l'Entreprise" (don't keyword stuff "Avocat pas cher")
- Category: Lawyer + Legal services
- Description: Use positioning sentence (FR): "Accompagnement juridique des entrepreneurs, freelances et e-commerçants : contrats, CGV, Loi 09-08, création de sociétés. Interventions à distance partout au Maroc. FR/AR/EN."
- Hours, photos of sober office, logo
- Posts hebdo: Share your LinkedIn article link (Google posts help SEO)
- Ask for reviews: After delivering pack, ask client (if Barreau allows, keep sober): "Un avis Google sobre m'aiderait à être visible pour d'autres entrepreneurs, sans mention comparative". If forbidden in your Barreau, skip reviews, focus on posts.

Local SEO: Even small city office can rank for "avocat [ville]" + national via articles.

## 2. Website: One-Page + Blog (Not 10 pages)

Use Carrd ($19/year) or Notion + Super or WordPress.

**Structure:**
- Home: Qui j'aide + 3 packs + Comment ça marche (3 étapes) + FAQ + Contact (WhatsApp button)
- No "publicité": Use informational tone.
- Blog: 1 article/week targeting keyword.

**Keywords to target (low competition, high intent):**

| Keyword (FR/EN) | Monthly searches | Intent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| modèle contrat freelance Maroc | 90-200 | High (want template → upsell) |
| CGV e-commerce Maroc loi 31-08 | 50-150 | Very High |
| loi 09-08 registre traitement modèle | 30-100 | Very High |
| créer SARL Maroc depuis France | 40-110 | High (MRE) |
| contrat prestation anglais français modèle | 70 | High |

Each keyword = one article (800-1200 words) + downloadable checklist.

**Article Template (SEO):**
- Title: "Modèle Contrat Freelance Maroc (FR/EN) : 12 clauses obligatoires en 2025"
- Intro: Pain + "Dans ce guide, je vous explique..."
- H2: Cadre légal Maroc
- H2: 12 clauses (list, with example phrase)
- H2: Erreurs à éviter
- H2: Modèle téléchargeable (Lead magnet → email)
- FAQ (schema)
- CTA doux: "Besoin d'une relecture de votre contrat ? Diagnostic 20 min"

**AI Prompt to draft:** "Rédige un article 1000 mots SEO sur [keyword] pour gérant PME marocain, ton clair, exemples pratiques, cadre loi marocaine 09-08/31-08, pas de pub directe, avec FAQ."

## 3. Lead Magnet Funnel (Capture)
Every article ends with: "Téléchargez la checklist PDF / Modèle Excel" → Tally Form → email/WhatsApp auto-send → sequence 3 emails/WhatsApp value → propose diagnostic.

Use Tally (free) + WhatsApp Business.

## 4. Timeline & Effort
- Month 1: 4 articles + GBP optimization
- Month 2: Google starts ranking (position 20-30)
- Month 3: 2-5 organic leads/month, then compounds.
- After 12 articles, you own the niche.

## 5. For Small City Advantage
You can rank nationally because nobody writes in ENGLISH about Moroccan law. Write 2 articles in English: "How to Hire a Freelancer in Morocco Legally" → capture foreign clients.
