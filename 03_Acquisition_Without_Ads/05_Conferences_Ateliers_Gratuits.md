# Acquisition #5: Ateliers Gratuits & Conférences (Most Compliant & High Trust)

> **This is the most "avocat-friendly" channel. Barreaux LOVE it. Highest conversion for small city.**

## Why Ateliers Win
- It's **information juridique gratuite**, not démarchage. 100% allowed, even encouraged.
- Small city advantage: CCI, Maison de l'Entrepreneur, co-workings are desperate for speakers. You fill room easily.
- Trust: People hire lawyers they have seen live, not a flyer.
- One atelier 90 min = 2-4 clients (5k-12k DH) vs 30 days of posting.

## Anatomy of a Perfect 60-Min Atelier

**0-5 min: Accueil + Cadre**
"Merci au [partenaire] pour l'accueil. Aujourd'hui: information juridique, pas de pub. Posez toutes vos questions."

**5-35 min: 3 Erreurs Coûteuses (Value)**
For freelance example:
1. Erreur 1: Pas de clause de propriété intellectuelle → exemple perdu 15k DH → Solution: phrase type
2. Erreur 2: Confusion AE/SARL → exemple blocage → Solution: tableau
3. Erreur 3: Pas de pénalité retard → exemple impayé → Solution: clause

Use slides Canva clean, 1 idea per slide, no jargon.

**35-50 min: Checklist Pratique**
Donner une checklist 1 page papier. They keep it, with your contact footer (allowed: informational footer).

**50-60 min: Q&A + Offre Douce**
"Si vous voulez appliquer ça à votre cas, je propose 5 diagnostics gratuits 20 min cette semaine (sur inscription). Laissez votre WhatsApp à la sortie."

No hard sell. Just invitation.

## How to Organize in Small City (Checklist)

- [ ] 1. Identify venue: CCI, coworking, salle fiduciaire, maison des jeunes, café with salle. Free or 300 DH HT.
- [ ] 2. Find co-host: 1 comptable + 1 association. They bring 10 people each, you bring 10 via LinkedIn/WhatsApp status.
- [ ] 3. Create simple affiche Canva A5 (sober, not "VENEZ VITE !!!"): Title + Date + Lieu + "Atelier gratuit - Places limitées 20 - Inscription WhatsApp"
- [ ] 4. Diffusion: WhatsApp status, LinkedIn post, ask co-host to share to their clients, FB group local. No paid ads.
- [ ] 5. Prepare: Slides 12 pages, checklist PDF printed, Tally form QR code for inscription.
- [ ] 6. Day J: Arrive early, test projector, bring paper + QR code.
- [ ] 7. J+1: Message all attendees WhatsApp: "Merci d'être venu. Voici le PDF checklist + lien pour diagnostic gratuit si tu veux."

## Conversion Math
- 20 attendees
- 12 leave WhatsApp (60%)
- 6 book diagnostic (50% of leads)
- 2 buy pack 3,500 DH HT (33% conversion) = 7,000 DH HT for 3h work.

Do 1x/month = 7k extra + authority + comptable relationship.
