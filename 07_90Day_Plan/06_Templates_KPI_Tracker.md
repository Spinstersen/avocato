# 06 — Templates & KPI Tracker (EN)

> What gets measured gets repeated. Track actions weekly, revenue monthly. Numbers drive the dopamine board, not feelings.

## TL;DR
- 5 numbers per week: outreach, diagnostics, packs signed, CA encaissé, referrals asked.
- Month 1 targets: 3 partners, 5 diagnostics, 2 packs, 15k DH CA.
- One tracker: Notion (or paper). Never two.

## The KPI sheet (copy into Notion)
| KPI | Target M1 | Target M2 | Target M3 | This week | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

## Monthly review (30 min, last day of month)
- Compare real vs target. No excuses, no punishment: adjust actions.
- If a KPI is at 0 two months in a row, the channel is wrong — switch one channel (`03_Acquisition_Without_Ads/`).
- Recalculate the average ticket: CA encaissé ÷ packs signed.
- Update pricing: `04_Skills_To_Learn/12_Finance_Cabinet_OS/01_Pricing_3_Options.md`.

## Rules
- [ ] Measure encaissé, not "signed".
- [ ] Max 7 KPIs. More = nothing gets tracked.
- [ ] Review on the calendar: Friday 16:00, non-negotiable.
- [ ] Visible board > perfect spreadsheet.
