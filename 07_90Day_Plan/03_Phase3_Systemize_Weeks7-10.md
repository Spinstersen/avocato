# Phase 3: Systemize (Weeks 7-10) — Make It Repeatable

> Goal: From hunting to farming. Build retainer + referrals.

## Focus

**Week 7: Retainer Offer**
- [ ] Create retainer 1-page PDF: "Juriste externalisé 2,500 DH HT/mo — 2 relectures + 1h Q&A + veille"
- [ ] Offer to past clients: "Tu as le pack, pour 2,500/mo je relis tes 2 prochains contrats, tu veux?"
- [ ] Aim: 1 retainer (2,500/mo = stable base)

**Week 8: Partnership Scale**
- [ ] Identify 5 more comptables (now you have proof: "J'ai aidé 5 freelances ce mois, voici leur cas anonymisé")
- [ ] Send 10 messages with social proof.
- [ ] Co-host 2nd atelier (same slides, new audience).

**Week 9: Content System + SEO**
- [ ] Publish SEO article #2 and #3 (now 3-4 total, Google starts traffic).
- [ ] Repurpose articles to LinkedIn carousels (no new writing).
- [ ] Setup WhatsApp Broadcast list + send 1 value broadcast/week.

**Week 10: Referral Engine Formalized**
- [ ] Create referral image Canva + QR.
- [ ] For every new client, add "Ask referral" to delivery checklist (automate).
- [ ] Ask past 5 clients for referrals again (they forgot).

## Metrics

- Packs: 4-6/month = 12k-18k DH
- Retainers: 1-2 = 2.5k-5k DH recurring
- Referrals: 3-5 leads/month free
- Partners active: 2 sending leads regularly

## System Improvements

- Refine Notion CRM: add automation (Tally → Notion via Make, 30 min setup)
- Refine templates: after 10 clients, you know common questions → add FAQ to Loom
- Raise price 10-15% after 8 clients (demand proof)

## ADHD Tip

Systemize = boring for ADHD. Make it game: "Can I close next client in 24h from diagnostic?" Challenge.
