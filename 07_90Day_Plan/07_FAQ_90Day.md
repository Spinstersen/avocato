# 07 — FAQ 90-Day (EN)

> Objections and real-life hiccups of the 90-day plan. Read only the line you need.

**Q: No client in month 1. Is the plan broken?**
A: Not yet. Month 1 is setup + outreach volume. Check the 7 grey zones of your niche (`02_Niches_Deep_Dive/[niche]/08_Zones_Grises_Cas_Limites.md`) and your message volume (100+ contacts). No income with less than 100 outreach is a volume problem, not a niche problem.

**Q: I only got 1 client instead of 3. Do I switch niche?**
A: No. One paying client is proof of concept. Finish the 90 days before judging (90-day rule).

**Q: Can I run 2 niches at once?**
A: No. Two niches = zero authority. One niche, one channel, 90 days.

**Q: Which niche first — freelancers or e-commerce?**
A: Whichever you already have 2-3 contacts in. Freelancers have the fastest EN cycle; e-commerce has the biggest volume.

**Q: I hate social media. Do I still need LinkedIn?**
A: No. Pick partnerships (comptables) instead — it is the #1 no-social channel.

**Q: How many hours per day?**
A: 2-3 focused hours (deep block + outreach block). The plan is designed for a practising lawyer, not a startup founder.

**Q: My diagnostic is free — is that OK?**
A: A free 20-min discovery is fine; the paid diagnostic (900 DH HT) converts better and filters. Discount it from the mission if signed within 14 days.

**Q: Clients say "too expensive".**
A: The diagnostic note shows the cost of the risk, not the price of your time. If 3 prospects in a row refuse with the same objection, rework the offer, not the price.

**Q: I missed a whole week. Restart?**
A: Never restart. Resume at the current phase with 1 MIT/day for 5 days (`06_ADHD_System/07_Recovery_Protocols.md`).

**Q: Do I need a website before starting?**
A: No. WhatsApp Business + LinkedIn/Google Business + 1 landing page is enough for the first 90 days.

**Q: When do I ask for referrals?**
A: At closing + 48h, on a delivered win. Never during delivery (`04_Skills_To_Learn/16_Client_Ops_Experience/07_Demander_le_Referral.md`).

**Q: Which content should I publish?**
A: One legal mechanism + one client mistake per post. No promises of result, no invented sanctions (loi 66-23 / déontologie).

**Q: The plan says 3 MITs/week but my week explodes.**
A: Keep 3, shrink each to 20 minutes. A moved MIT is fine; a deleted MIT is a decision to make at the weekly review.

**Q: What does "done" look like at J90?**
A: 5-10 active clients (packs + retainers), one channel producing regularly, and a written decision for the next quarter.

**Q: Where do I put the numbers?**
A: One page: `06_Templates_KPI_Tracker.md`. Review Friday 16:00.
