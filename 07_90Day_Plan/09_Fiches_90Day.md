# 09 — Fiches 90-Day (EN)

> The 12 sprints in one page. Print this file and fill it weekly.

## The 12 weekly sprints
| Sprint | Week | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | W1 | Setup: niche, positioning, offer | Profiles updated, 1 pack defined |
| 2 | W2 | Setup: lead magnet, partners list, first outreach | 1 lead magnet, 15 contacts |
| 3 | W3 | Outreach + first diagnostics | 5 diagnostics booked |
| 4 | W4 | First mission delivery + content | 1 pack delivered |
| 5 | W5 | Referral + second mission | 1 referral asked |
| 6 | W6 | Atelier/workshop #1 | 1 atelier run |
| 7 | W7 | Retention: retainer offer | 1 retainer proposed |
| 8 | W8 | Systemize: SOP, batching | 2 SOPs written |
| 9 | W9 | Partners activation | 3 active partners |
| 10 | W10 | Content compounding | 6 articles/posts live |
| 11 | W11 | Second niche test (optional) | 1 diagnostic in new niche |
| 12 | W12 | J90 review + next 90 plan | Written decision |
