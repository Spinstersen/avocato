# 01 — Catalogue Complet 15 Outils

## La stack en 3 couches

### Couche 1 — Cœur (J1 obligatoire)
- **Notion Plus $10** — Second brain, CRM, missions, content. Voir `02_Notion_Cabinet_OS_Detaille.md`.
- **Google Workspace $6** — Email pro `prenom@cabinet.ma`, Drive, Meet. DKIM/SPF obligatoire.
- **Calendly/Cal.com $10** — 3 types d'événements: Diagnostic 45m (payant), Suivi 30m, Atelier 60m. Buffer 15m, confirmation auto, annulation 24h.

### Couche 2 — Production (Semaine 2)
- **Tally Gratuit** — Intake prospect (12 questions), feedback. Alternative RGPD: Fillout EU.
- **Yousign €25** — Templates conventions, workflow envoi→signature→archivage Notion. QES si besoin tribunal.
- **Loom Business $12.5** — 3 templates vidéo (voir `05_Loom_Video_Client.md`).
- **Canva Pro $15** — Brand Kit Navy #0F2A44 / Beige #F5F1E8 / Teal #1FA89E.
- **Plausible $9** — Analytics sans bandeau cookie, hébergé UE.

### Couche 3 — Optimisation (Mois 2)
- **Stripe** — Si RIO tolère (Casablanca tolérant, Rabat strict). Sinon virement + reçu provision manuel `05_Document_Bank/templates/06_Recu_Provision_Facture.md`.
- **Zapier/Make $20** — Tally→Notion, Calendly→Google→Notion, Yousign→Drive.
- **Freedom $3.3** — Bloque LinkedIn/YouTube pendant Deep Work.
- **Brain.fm $7** — Focus music ADHD.
- **Whisper/Notion AI** — Transcription voice notes.
