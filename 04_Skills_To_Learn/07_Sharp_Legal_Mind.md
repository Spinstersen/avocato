# Sharpness Curriculum — What to Learn to Stay Sharp

> Sharp = legal substance + commercial instinct + mental clarity. 3 pillars, 30 min/day, all ADHD-proof. No 500-page books.

## TL;DR
Sharpness = (1) master 3 legal domains deeply, not 10 shallowly, (2) read judgment/decisions weekly so you cite real cases, (3) train reasoning daily with small exercises + stay current on tech/business. 30 min/day beats 3 hours on Sunday.

---

## PILLAR 1 — LEGAL MASTERY (the substance clients pay for)

### Your 3 mastery zones (deep not wide)
1. **Droit des contrats (DOC + clauses pratiques):** contrat de prestation, CGV, clause pénale, réserve de propriété, résiliation, force majeure
2. **Statut de l'entreprise marocaine:** AE, SARL, SARL-AU, SA, EURL (création, fiscalité simple, responsabilités)
3. **Loi 09-08 / 31-08 (consommateur + données):** CGV obligations, rétractation, CNDP, registre

Plus a radar on: droit du travail (embauche 1er salarié), paiement international (droit cambiste, réserve), marques OMPIC.

### Weekly legal routine (30 min, 2x/week)
- **Mardi:** read 1-2 new CNDP guides / délibérations (cndp.ma) + 1 article loi 09-08 contexte
- **Jeudi:** read 1 case/decision (barreau publications, jep.ma — jurisprudence en ligne, Cass. com.) + note "ce que ça change pour mes clients" in Notion
- **Monthly:** re-read ONE full law text in your niche (ex: loi 09-08, 80 pages, in 3 sessions of 30 min — not in one sitting)

### The "client-facing sharpness" test
After each reading, ask: "How do I turn this into 1 sentence a client understands + 1 risk number?" Write the answer in Notion "Learning" page. If you can't, you haven't learned it yet.

## PILLAR 2 — THINKING SKILLS (reasoning, argument, speed)

### 1. Issue-spotting (the lawyer superpower)
Daily 5-min exercise (like a game):
> Open any business news (LeBrief, Médias24, MarocHebdo). Find ONE legal risk in the story. Example: "une startup lève 5M DH" → risks: pacte d'associés? dilution? actionnaires minoritaires?
Write the risk in 2 lines. After 30 days you see risks everywhere = clients feel your sharpness instantly.

### 2. The S-C-Q-A argument structure (make every opinion sharp)
- **S**ituation (1 line facts)
- **C**omplication (what's wrong/dangerous)
- **Q**uestion (what must be decided)
- **A**nswer (your position + 2 reasons)
Practice: explain any legal point to a friend in SCQA format. You'll sound like a senior counsel, not a student.

### 3. Deal-reasoning (10 min/day, the fun one)
Weekly case from `02_Niches_Deep_Dive/` — take a client scenario and answer: "What's the best structure? What's the risk? What would I charge?" Type it in 150 words. This builds pricing + advice speed.

### 4. Numbers literacy
Clients respect precise figures: AE plafonds, TVA, frais CRI, délais CNDP, amende 31-08. Make a "Numbers sheet" in Notion (10 key numbers), memorize 5, update when they change (ex: AE plafonds 2025/2026). Numbers = instant authority.

## PILLAR 3 — BUSINESS & TECH AWARENESS (your differentiator vs other lawyers)

30 min/day rotating (ADHD-friendly variety):
- **Lundi:** tech business news (Stripe news, Shopify updates, YouCan announcements) — know what clients use
- **Mercredi:** AI tools test (1 new tool/week, 20 min): not legal advice, but organization, research (Perplexity), drafting
- **Vendredi:** read 1 founder interview / business podcast (EN or FR) — understand their world, their words
- **Daily:** scan 10 min of your niche's Facebook groups — see their real questions in their real words (gold for content)

## ADHD RULES
- **Variety is your friend:** rotate subjects daily (above) — novelty = dopamine = consistency
- **Shallow depth rule:** read summaries, not full books. Depth comes from repetition over months, not one sitting.
- **Output > input:** always write 2-3 lines after reading (Notion). Reading without output is entertainment.
- **Deadline trick:** tell your partner/comptable friend "vendredi je t'explique [sujet] en 5 min" — teaching under promise = focus.
