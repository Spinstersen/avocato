# 06 — Weekly Review & Planning (EN)

> Sunday, 30 minutes, same slot. The week is won on Sunday, not Monday morning.

## TL;DR
- One 30-min review: wins, misses, next 3 MITs, one improvement.
- Numbers first (diagnostics, packs, CA), feelings after.
- Archive and close the week in Notion; empty the head before Monday.

## Agenda (30 min, timer visible)
| Min | Block | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Wins** | 3 things that worked (write them, dopamine) |
| 5–10 | **Numbers** | Diagnostics, packs signed, CA encaissé, messages sent |
| 10–15 | **Misses** | What slipped and why (no blame, one line each) |
| 15–20 | **Next 3 MITs** | The 3 client/revenue actions for next week |
| 20–25 | **Content** | 3 post titles for the week |
| 25–30 | **One improvement** | Change ONE thing (calendar, script, template) |

## Weekly sprint structure (repeat)
- Monday 10:00 — plan the 3 MITs for the week.
- Tuesday/Thursday 11:00 — outreach block (5 messages each).
- Sunday 10:00–12:00 — batch content.
- Friday 16:00 — mini review + reward.

## Notion archive (5 min, do it or it piles up)
- Move "Done" tasks to the week's archive page.
- Close the week's page with 3 bullets: what worked, what drained, what to change.
- Update the KPI tracker (`07_90Day_Plan/06_Templates_KPI_Tracker.md`).
- Dopamine board: mark the visible wins (clients, posts, diagnostics).

## Questions to ask every week
1. How many diagnostics booked? How many packs signed?
2. Which channel produced the last client? (Do more of that one.)
3. What drained me that I can delete or delegate?
4. Am I still on ONE niche and ONE channel? (ADHD drift check.)

## Checklist
- [ ] Wins written
- [ ] Numbers logged
- [ ] 3 MITs for next week chosen
- [ ] 3 content titles drafted
- [ ] Week archived in Notion
- [ ] Reward taken
