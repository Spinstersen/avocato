# 07 — Recovery Protocols (EN)

> A bad day is data, not failure. The system only breaks if you reset the streak — so never reset it.

## TL;DR
- Missed 3 days? Next day = 15 minutes, then continue. No "start over".
- 3R protocol: Breathe 5m → Reduce scope 15m → Re-schedule.
- Minimum Viable Day (45 min) keeps the streak alive during crises.

## 3R protocol (when the day collapses)
1. **Breathe (5 min)** — 4-7-8 or box breathing, phone away. The panic is physiological before it's strategic.
2. **Reduce (15 min)** — pick the ONE task that pays (send the devis, answer the client, sign the contract). Drop the rest on purpose.
3. **Re-schedule (5 min)** — move the dropped MITs to a real slot tomorrow, in writing. Unwritten tasks become guilt.

## Minimum Viable Day (MVD) — 45 min total
- 15 min: the revenue action (message, call, devis).
- 15 min: one client deliverable.
- 15 min: shutdown + 3 MITs for tomorrow.

That's a complete day. Everything above is bonus, not baseline.

## After missing days
- Do NOT rebuild a perfect week. Do 15 min the next day, then normal.
- One miss = noise. Two = signal to shrink the system. Three = cut a commitment (client, channel, project).
- No re-reading the whole vault, no new app, no new plan. Same plan, smaller Sunday.

## Burnout signals (from `01_Strategy/05_ADHD_Operating_System_Overview/08_Protocoles_Recuperation.md`)
- Three bad nights in a row, irritability with clients, dread opening WhatsApp.
- Response: 48h with one MVD/day, no outreach, delivery only. Then resume.
- If signals persist 2 weeks: reduce the offer ladder to ONE pack and ONE retainer.

## Rules
- [ ] Never zero a day (MVD counts).
- [ ] Never "restart" the system — resume where it paused.
- [ ] Guilt is banned from the review: it changes behavior worse than metrics do.
- [ ] Ask for help early: confrère, assistant, VA (`04_Skills_To_Learn/17_Operations_SOP_Scaling/`).
