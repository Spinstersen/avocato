# 10 — FAQ ADHD (EN)

> Short answers to the questions that kill momentum. Read the one you need, close the file.

**Q: I missed 3 days. Do I restart?**
A: No. Do 15 minutes today, then continue. The streak resumes, it never resets (`07_Recovery_Protocols.md`).

**Q: How many MITs?**
A: Three. Revenue, visibility, delivery. A fourth task means the first three are too big.

**Q: What if I can't finish the deep-work block?**
A: Do one 25-min cycle. It counts. Stop at the timer, not at perfection.

**Q: I have 47 ideas. What do I do with them?**
A: Brain-dump them in Notion "Ideas". They wait there. Only one niche and one channel for 90 days.

**Q: The admin is piling up. When do I do it?**
A: Two windows only: 11:30–12:00 and 18:00–18:30. Outside them, notifications off.

**Q: Should I take a client I don't like?**
A: If it pays the target and doesn't pull you off the niche, take it — but log the time it steals. Three bad-fit clients in a row = wrong niche.

**Q: I skipped the reward. Is that OK?**
A: No. The reward is fuel, not decoration. Take the small one after a 3-MIT day.

**Q: My phone defeats every system.**
A: Phone in another room during the block, Forest running, WhatsApp windows only. If it still wins, use a kitchen timer and leave the phone at home when you can.

**Q: How do I know the system is working?**
A: Diagnostics booked, packs signed, CA encaissé (the dopamine board). Vanity metrics don't count.

**Q: I failed a whole week. Now what?**
A: Sunday review, smaller plan: 1 MIT/day for the next week. No new tools, no new niche, no reset.

**Q: Can I adapt the schedule?**
A: Yes — move the blocks, keep the structure: one deep block, two shallow windows, one review. Structure survives, hours change.

**Q: What if a client wants an urgent call during deep work?**
A: Answer within 2 hours with a concrete slot. Urgent is not immediate. You are a lawyer, not a hotline.

**Q: I'm bored by the niche after 3 weeks.**
A: Boredom is not evidence. Read the 90-day rule (`01_Strategy/03_Unsaturated_Niches_Overview/00_INDEX.md`): pivot at J90 with numbers, not at J21 with feelings.

**Q: Should I work in the evening?**
A: Low energy after 17:00 = admin, Canva, scheduling. Drafting and contracts belong in the 10:00 block.

**Q: What is the single most important rule?**
A: Never zero a day. 45 minutes beats a perfect plan you didn't start.
