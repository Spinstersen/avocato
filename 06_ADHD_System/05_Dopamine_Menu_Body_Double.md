# 05 — Dopamine Menu & Body Double (EN)

> ADHD brains don't run on discipline, they run on fuel. Stock the menu BEFORE the crash.

## TL;DR
- Build a 4-level dopamine menu and pick from it without thinking.
- Body doubling turns "I should" into "we're doing it now".
- Rewards must be immediate, small and NOT the thing you're avoiding.

## The 4-level menu (write yours on a card)
| Level | Cost | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** (5 min) | Free, instant | 5m walk, glass of water, stretch, 1 song, tidy 1 surface |
| **Small** (15–30 min) | Cheap | tea/coffee outside, episode, call a friend, 20m nap |
| **Medium** (1–2 h) | Planned | cinema, hammam, game session, dinner out |
| **Large** (half-day) | Earned | weekend trip, big purchase, full day off (no laptop) |

Rule: micro after each pomodoro set, medium after a full 3-MIT day, large after a 5-day streak.

## When to use what
- Can't start → **micro** (2 min movement) then a 25-min timer.
- Mid-block dip → **micro** (stand/walk, no screen).
- Finished 2 MITs, zero fuel → **small**, then the 3rd MIT.
- End of a good week → **large**, guilt-free. This is the point of the system.

## Body doubling (the ADHD cheat code)
- **Focusmate / Flown**: 25/50-min sessions with a stranger, camera on, goal stated out loud.
- **Café/library**: go with ONE task and headphones; ordering is the entry ritual.
- **"Study with me"** video on a second screen for admin work.
- **With someone**: share a 45-min timer, silent work, 5-min chat at the end.
- State the goal before starting: "By the end of this session I will have sent 5 DMs." Vague session = vague result.

## Guardrails
- [ ] Reward never = scrolling (it extends and never ends).
- [ ] Menu written on paper, visible from the desk.
- [ ] One large reward/week maximum — otherwise it's not a reward.
- [ ] If you skip the reward, the system dies. Take it.
