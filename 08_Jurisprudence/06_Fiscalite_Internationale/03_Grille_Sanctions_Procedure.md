## Grille sanctions / procedure chiffree

| Manquement | Base | Sanction / Delai / Cout |
|---|---|---|
| Residence 183j/foyer | CGI23 + Conv4 | Attestation DGI 30j |
| PE 7 mois | Conv7+5 | FR 25% + Maroc15% - credit |
| Prix transfert 1k vs 5k | CGI213 | Redress 48k +30% |
| Dividende FR 10% | Conv10 | 5000-F + credit |

## Procedure 30 min diagnostic

1. Check 5 min checklist (voir 04_Phrase_Diagnostic).
2. Si 2 cases non cochees -> mission recommandee (pack HT + provision 50%).
3. Delai livrable 7j apres provision + pieces (CNDP 30j, OMPIC 72h, Office 30j).
4. Archive SWIFT/facture/RC 10 ans.

## Couts indicatifs

Voir 01_Texte_Source chiffres PLF. Ex: IS 15% <300k benef, OMPIC 1.200/classe, CNSS 27.83%, dotation 15k/100k, CNDP 0 declaration 30j.

## Interactions

Sans 03 rap 30j -> 04 TVA92 perdue. Sans 01 registre -> 04 Office+05 PI si logo visage donnee perso.
