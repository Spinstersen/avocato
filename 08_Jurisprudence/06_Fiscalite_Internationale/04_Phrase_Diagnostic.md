## Phrase diagnostic 30 sec (FR sobre — a dire)

> "Foyer Casa + 40j = resident Maroc art.4 meme 40j. 1 salarie Paris 7 mois signe = PE 20k surcout. Dividende 10% FR si 5000-F sinon 15% perte 5k. On teste 3 questions 5 min."

## Checklist 5 min (a cocher en diagnostic)

- [ ] Base legale presente (registre/CGV/contrat/facture/marque/attestation) ?
- [ ] Delai respecte (45j CNDP, 7j retract, 30j Office, 72h OMPIC, 48h AT) ?
- [ ] Preuve archivee (SWIFT, RC, ICE, passeport, PV) ?
- [ ] Si 2 non -> mission pack HT provision 50% art.30
