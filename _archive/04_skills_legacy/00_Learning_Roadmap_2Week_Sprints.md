# Learning Roadmap — 2-Week Sprints (No Overwhelm)

> Don't learn everything. Learn just enough to sell next pack. Each sprint = 5h total (30 min/day).

## Sprint 1 (Weeks 1-2): Sell Freelance Pack
- Loi 31-08 (protection consommateur) basics: CGV obligations
- DOC contrat prestation, clause pénale, réserve propriété
- AE vs SARL 2025 plafonds, différences
- Resource: Code des Obligations et Contrats (DOC) Titre II + site OMPIC guide SARL
- Output: Checklist 12 clauses + contrat template v1

## Sprint 2 (Weeks 3-4): Sales & Loom (MAIN)
- SPIN questions + Challenger teach + anchoring (see `03_Sales_Without_Selling/01_Methodes_Vente.md`)
- Record yourself 3x (15 min/day), fix 1 thing each time
- French client communication week 1: LanguageTool + rewrite email templates (`06_French_Communication_With_Clients.md`)
- Loom + Canva basics
- Resource: SPIN explainer video (15 min) + "Getting to Yes" summary (20 min) + "Loom for lawyers" YouTube
- Output: 1 Loom demo + devis design + 3 email templates rewritten in clean French

## Sprint 3 (Weeks 5-6): E-com or 09-08 (pick one)
- If Ecom: Loi 31-08 détaillé + YouCan/Shopify CGV placement + contrat fournisseur
- If 09-08: Loi 09-08 + délibération CNDP 40-22 + modèle registre CNDP
- Resource: CNDP.ma guides + Loi 09-08 PDF (80 pages, read summary 10 pages only)
- Output: Quiz conformité or registre Excel

## Sprint 4 (Weeks 7-8): SEO & LinkedIn
- SEO basics (keyword, H2, meta)
- LinkedIn carousel design
- Resource: YouTube "SEO for beginners 2024" 1h + Canva tutorial 30 min
- Output: 2 SEO articles + 3 carousels

## Sprint 5 (Weeks 9-10): Automation & Retainer
- Notion automation, Zapier/Make basics
- How to price retainer, DPO role
- Resource: Notion official tutorials 1h
- Output: Retainer PDF + Notion CRM automation

## Sprint 6 (Weeks 11-12): Sharpness Loop
- Issue-spotting daily (5 min game from `07_Sharp_Legal_Mind.md`)
- Weekly jurisprudence + CNDP reading routine (30 min x2)
- Numbers sheet (10 key figures memorized)
- French oral sharpening: voice note practice 3x/week + 1 recorded diagnostic analyzed with AI
- Output: Notion "Learning" page running + Numbers sheet done

## Constant Tracks (run in background, 15-30 min/day)
These never stop — rotate daily, they compound:
- **Lundi:** tech/business news scan (Stripe, Shopify, YouCan) → content ideas
- **Mardi/Jeudi:** legal reading (CNDP, jurisprudence) + client-facing note
- **Mercredi:** new AI tool test
- **Vendredi:** founder podcast/read + niche FB groups scan
- **Daily:** French: LanguageTool on all client writing; record voice notes on legal topics

See `06_French_Communication_With_Clients.md` (words) + `07_Sharp_Legal_Mind.md` (substance) for details.

## How to Learn ADHD-Friendly
- 30 min/day max, after Deep Work (not before, else procrastination)
- Active: Take notes by hand, then test by explaining to friend/voice note.
- Just-in-time: Don't read Loi 09-08 fully if you sell Freelance pack now. Read when you need.
- Store notes in Notion "Learning" page, 3 bullets per resource.
